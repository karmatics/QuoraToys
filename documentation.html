1. Continuous 360° Callout Frame & Wide Corner Geometry

Widened Dual-Edge Invariant ($15^\circ \to 75^\circ$):

The callout bracket never displays two parallel edges. It displays either a single vertical edge, a single horizontal edge, or both perpendicular edges meeting at the arrival corner across a generous angular sector.

  • Trajectory Vector: $\mathbf{v} = P_{\text{end}} - P_{\text{cp}}$, $\alpha = \operatorname{atan2}(v_y, v_x)$.
  • Near Horizontal ($0^\circ \to 15^\circ$): Displays only the arrival vertical edge (Left or Right), connecting at $H/2$ for pure horizontal.
  • Near Vertical ($75^\circ \to 90^\circ$): Displays only the arrival horizontal edge (Top or Bottom), connecting at $W/2$ for pure vertical.
  • Wide Dual-Edge Zone ($15^\circ \to 75^\circ$): Both perpendicular edges display simultaneously as an L-bracket, meeting seamlessly at the corner with zero gap.
  • Zero Gap Guarantee: $(p_x + c_x, p_y + c_y) = P_{\text{end}}$ holds everywhere along 360°.

2. Vibrant Saturated Glow & Independent Optics

Decoupling Text Glow from Curve Glow:

  • Rich Saturated Text Glow: Text glyphs maintain vibrant, authentic color instead of washing out to 88% pale white.
  • Independent Filter Controls: Curve glow blur/width and text glow blur/saturation are adjustable independently.
  • Floating Background Option: By default, callout text floats transparently with no dark bounding box. A backdrop opacity slider allows custom darkening when needed.
