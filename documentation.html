1. Continuous 360° Callout Frame & Angular Connection Specification

Strict Frame Invariant (One or Two Edges Only):

The callout bracket never displays two parallel edges (e.g., top and bottom together). It displays either a single vertical edge, a single horizontal edge, or two perpendicular edges meeting at the arrival corner.

  • Trajectory Vector: $\mathbf{v} = P_{\text{end}} - P_{\text{cp}}$, $\alpha = \operatorname{atan2}(v_y, v_x)$.
  • $\pm 30^\circ$ of Cardinal Horizontal: Displays only the arrival vertical edge (Left or Right). The contact point sits at the dead center ($H/2$) for pure horizontal, sliding smoothly toward the corner as $\alpha$ tilts.
  • $\pm 30^\circ$ of Cardinal Vertical: Displays only the arrival horizontal edge (Top or Bottom). The contact point sits at the dead center ($W/2$) for pure vertical, sliding toward the corner as $\alpha$ tilts.
  • $30^\circ \text{ to } 60^\circ$ Corner Zone: Both perpendicular edges turn on, meeting at the corner point $(0, 0)$, $(W, 0)$, $(0, H)$, or $(W, H)$ at exactly $\pm 45^\circ$ or $\pm 135^\circ$.
  • Zero Gap Guarantee: $(p_x + c_x, p_y + c_y) = P_{\text{end}}$ everywhere along the full 360° range.

2. Unified Hybrid Pen Architecture & Gesture Machine

Eliminating Modal Switching Between "Click" and "Drag":

  • A pale deadzone circle of radius $R$ ($32\text{px}$) appears with a fat white point indicator at $P_0$.
  • Click Release Inside $R$: Registers as a discrete knot insertion, entering point-by-point mode with live rubber-banding.
  • Drag Exceeding $R$: Automatically engages the solid white drawstring tether. The pen tip trails at $P_{\text{pen}} = \text{Cursor} - \mathbf{u} \cdot R$.

3. Tangency Ray Synchronization Protocol

Zero-Artifact Drag Synchronization:

  • Dragging an attached callout text box moves the endpoint knot of the curve directly under the cursor.
  • Any stale outbound tangent ray (#qa-outbound-tangent-ray) is destroyed at the start of drag, preventing it from being left behind in mid-air.
  • On drag release, exposeBezierHandles() regenerates the knot marker and outbound ray at the new coordinates.
